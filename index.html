Guang Tian

PhD Student in Condensed Matter Physics · Peking University (PKU)
PhD student in Prof. Xiaosong Wu's group at Peking University (PKU). I study nonlinear and Hall transport in topological and magnetic quantum materials using low-temperature electrical, magnetic, and thermal measurements.

Collaboration: Open to research collaborations in quantum transport and magnetic topological materials.
View publications Email GitHub RedNote ResearchGate

Research Focus

Nonlinear Quantum Transport
Third-order and magneto-nonlinear responses linked to quantum geometry and disorder.
Hall Effects in Topological Magnets
Anomalous and in-plane Hall transport in kagome and Weyl magnetic materials.
Low-Temperature Magnetotransport
Electrical and magnetic measurements across temperature, field, and angle.
Thermal Transport
Thermal probes of electronic and magnetic excitations in quantum materials.
